## 1. What we collect

## 2. Legal basis and consent
We process personal data with your explicit consent, given when you create an account and when you
start each recording. You confirm you have the teacher's permission before recording a course. You can
withdraw consent at any time by deleting a lecture or your account in the app.

else is private to you. We never publish recordings.
